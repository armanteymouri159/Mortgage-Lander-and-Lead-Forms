Common questions

Will my current mortgage change?
No. Your mortgage stays exactly the same. A HELOC is a separate line of credit based on your equity, layered on top.
How much might I access?
Often up to a portion of your home's value minus what you still owe. The exact figure is confirmed during your check.
Do I need perfect credit?
Not perfect — there are minimums, but some programs weigh your actual deposits and cash flow alongside credit.
Is there a traditional appraisal?
Often no. Many programs use automated valuation models, which is a big reason the process moves so fast.
Can I get a fixed rate?
Fixed-rate options are commonly available, giving you a predictable payment.
How fast is funding?
Some qualified homeowners see funds in as little as 3–5 days. Timelines vary and are not guaranteed.
